Coming soon: inspections with fewer blind spots.

CMP Verified Inspections is being designed to give landlords a clearer, more reliable view of property condition between visits, using live in-app capture, location checks, timestamps and guided room-by-room prompts.

  • Guided in-app camera flow with no simple upload of old camera-roll photos.
  • Room-by-room prompts for walls, floors, ceilings, windows, doors, fixtures, damp and mould risks, visible damage and maintenance issues.
  • Red-to-green coverage concept using date, time, location and device/session metadata.
  • LiDAR-enhanced on compatible iPhone Pro and iPad Pro devices.
