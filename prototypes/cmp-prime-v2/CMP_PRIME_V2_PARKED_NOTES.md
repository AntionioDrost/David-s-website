## What This Version Demonstrates

CMP Prime V2 is the A-Z modular compliance checker direction. It demonstrates the main CMP foundation:

- route intent selection;
- address selection;
- scenario questions;
- A-Z Compliance Map;
- Property Plan workspace;
- Fix First action;
- service booking;
- Smart Document Scan;
- Ask CMP;
- human support;
- possession preparation preview;
- My Properties.

## Good Enough To Keep

- The A-Z Compliance Map gives CMP a clear product foundation.
- The Property Plan ties requirements, evidence, services and monitoring into one landlord-facing workflow.
- The Fix First pattern gives the landlord one practical next action instead of a flat checklist.
- Service booking is connected to actual gaps rather than presented as a separate marketplace.
- Smart Document Scan is useful without overclaiming legal or AI verification.
- Possession preparation is visible and commercially important while staying advisor-supported.
- The reviewer path from the homepage now reaches the example Property Plan directly.
- The current branch has a Netlify draft preview for manual review.

## Known Future Improvement Ideas

- Add real route URLs or hash states so reviewers can link directly to specific screens.
- Add stronger visual hierarchy for the A-Z Compliance Map once the product direction is chosen.
- Expand portfolio handling beyond a single active property.
- Add richer renewal and reminder detail.
- Replace the static address and EPC examples with real integrations when the product direction is ready.
- Add more robust browser smoke coverage for the main reviewer paths.
- Tighten mobile ergonomics after the preferred prototype direction is selected.

## Reason For Pausing Now

This version is strong enough to preserve as the A-Z modular compliance checker option. It proves the core CMP foundation without needing another redesign pass before exploring a different product direction.
